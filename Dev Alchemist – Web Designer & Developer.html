TLA Academy

Problem

A digital academy needed one place for students to learn and for administrators to run courses.

What I did

Designed and built the academy site with a sign-in screen that sends students and administrators to separate portals. Students get courses, progress and certificates. Admins manage courses, modules and lessons. New students can create an account from the same screen.

02 — Forex trading website

The Alchemists Trading Cooperation

Problem

A Forex trading business needed a website that builds trust with traders and turns visitors into VIP members.

What I did

Designed and built a dark, green-accented site around a bold headline, a trading chart visual, and a clear Join VIP button. It covers About, Services, VIP, Results, Mentorship, FAQ and Contact, with Chat and Telegram shortcuts for quick questions.

03 — Personal celebration site

E&J Anniversary Website

Problem

A couple wanted a memorable way to celebrate their first anniversary and share their story.

What I did

Designed and built a romantic story site that opens with a large monogram, soft pink and purple glow, and floating hearts on a dark background. Dot navigation on the side lets visitors move through the story one section at a time.

About

I’m Dev Alchemist, an independent web designer and developer. I build websites that look sharp, work properly on every screen and give a business a clear, credible presence online.

I care about the details people feel before they can explain them: spacing, type, and how a page reads on a phone. Every project starts with understanding what you need, then turns into something made carefully, from design through to launch.

Design philosophy

Modern, functional and visually engaging. Clear hierarchy, careful spacing and type that carries the message.

Development approach

Fast, responsive builds that work properly on phones, tablets and desktops, made with attention to detail.
